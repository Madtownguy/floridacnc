Privacy policy

Pastries and more! Updated November 22, 2026.

No data is collected

This game does not collect data. There is no account, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. The iPhone app does not make network calls. It plays from files stored on the phone and does not open floridacnc.com.

What stays on the device

The baker name you type, and the bakery progress, stay on the device. On the iPhone they are saved inside the app. On the website they are saved in the browser. They are not sent to Josh, to Florida CNC, or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser’s saved data for floridacnc.com, removes that save. The website save and the iPhone save are separate.

Children

Pastries and more! is a game for a child. It does not ask for an email, a phone number, a photo, or a location. The baker name stays on the device.
