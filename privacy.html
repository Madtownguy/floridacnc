Privacy policy

Shop Floor, Dead Acre, Pastries and more!, and floridacnc.com. Updated November 22, 2026.

Who this covers

This policy is for the website floridacnc.com and for three games published by Josh Butz: Shop Floor, Dead Acre, and Pastries and more!. The games are on the website and, when you install them from the Apple App Store, inside the iPhone apps. Pastries and more! also has a short privacy page for its App Store listing.

What stays on your device

The games save progress on the device you are playing on. Shop Floor remembers your shop. Dead Acre remembers scores, waves, and settings. Pastries and more! remembers the bakery and the baker name. That save stays in the browser when you play on the website, and inside the app when you play the iPhone version. It is there the next time you open the game on that same device. It is not sent to Josh or to anyone else.

Deleting the app, or clearing the browser’s saved data for floridacnc.com, removes that save. The website save and the iPhone save are separate.

What is not collected

The games have no account, no ads, and no in-app purchases. They do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or photos. They do not send gameplay or a device identifier to Josh, to Florida CNC, or to an analytics service.

The iPhone apps run from files stored inside the app. Opening an app does not load floridacnc.com.

The quote form on floridacnc.com is separate from the games. If you send a quote request, the name, company, city, email, phone, and message you type are delivered to Josh so he can reply. That form is not part of Shop Floor, Dead Acre, or Pastries and more!.

Children

The games do not ask children for personal information. There is nothing to sign up for. Pastries and more! does not ask a child for an email, a phone number, a photo, or a location. The baker name stays on the device.
