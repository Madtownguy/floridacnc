About FANUC ROBOCUT

FANUC calls the ROBOCUT α-CiC series a high-reliability, high-performance wire electrical-discharge machine, offered in three table sizes: α-C400iC, α-C600iC and α-C800iC.

Per FANUC America, the AWF3 automatic wire feed threads wire in about 10 seconds, the machine re-threads automatically in the wire path on workpieces up to 150 mm thick, and twin-servo wire tension control holds tension to ±15 g.
