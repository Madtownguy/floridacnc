About Nakamura-Tome

Tomeo Nakamura founded Nakamura-Tome Iron Works in Japan in 1949; today the company is Nakamura-Tome Precision Industry Co., Ltd. (Source: Nakamura-Tome company history.)

Nakamura-Tome builds ATC multitasking machines with its compact “NT Smart Cube” tool spindle, plus two-spindle machines with two or three turrets for simultaneous upper and lower machining.

New from Nakamura-Tome (July 2026): WT-150V: two turrets, two spindles, milling spindles 6,000 rpm (10,000 optional). Read more

Key models

A few current models. Specs are copied from the manufacturer pages linked on each card; check the latest brochure before ordering.

  • JX-50

    ATC multitasking machine

    • Bar capacity ø42 mm, 5 or 6 in. chucks
    • NT Smart Cube tool spindle up to 30,000 min⁻¹
    • 60 tools standard, up to 119 (option)
    • 3.3 s chip to chip
    Specs on nakamura-tome.com →

  • JX-200

    ATC multitasking machine

    • Left bar capacity 65 mm (80 mm option)
    • 80-tool ATC magazine (40 or 120 option)
    • Lower turret with milling and Y-axis standard
    • Distance between spindles up to 1,250 mm
    Specs on nakamura-tome.com →

  • NT-Flex

    Two-spindle, two-turret multitasking lathe

    • 24-station upper and lower turrets, up to 96 tools
    • Bar capacity 32 mm (38 mm option)
    • 8,000 min⁻¹ spindles, 5 in. chucks
    Specs on nakamura-tome.com →

  • WT-150II

    Two-spindle, two-turret turning center

    • Bar capacity 51 mm (65 mm left only)
    • Left spindle 15/11 kW, right 11/7.5 kW
    • Up to 48 tools
    Specs on nakamura-tome.com →

  • NTY³-100V

    Three-turret, two-spindle multitasking machine

    • Up to 72 tool stations
    • Bar capacity 42, 51 or 65 mm
    • 6 in. chucks
    Specs on nakamura-tome.com →
