About OKK

OKK traces its history to 1915, when it was established in Osaka as Matsuda Seisakusho to make centrifugal pumps. It is now part of the Nidec group as Nidec OKK. (Source: Nidec OKK corporate history.)

Nidec's machine tool range includes OKK horizontal machining centers (HM series), VC-X 5-axis machining centers and VP-series vertical machining centers.

OKK availability can depend on region. Ask Josh about Florida.
