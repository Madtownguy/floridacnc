About YASDA

YASDA was founded in 1929 in Osaka, Japan, entered the machine tool industry in 1964 with the “Jig Master” horizontal boring and milling machine, and developed its first horizontal machining center in 1966. (Source: YASDA Precision America.)

Yasda Precision America Corp., established in 1997, supports YASDA customers in the USA and names Methods Machine Tools as its exclusive U.S. partner.
