About KIWA

KIWA Machinery is based in Nabari, Mie, Japan. It started manufacturing lathes in 1933, horizontal machining centers in 1996 and a 5-axis vertical machining center in 2008. (Source: KIWA company history.)

KIWA's horizontal machining centers are known for narrow machine widths (the KH-4100kai is 1,530 mm wide), and its Triple-series machines use a column-traverse structure with a fixed table.
